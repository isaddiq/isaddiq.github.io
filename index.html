About Me

Most recently, I was a researcher at buildingSMART Korea, where I worked on Human and Robot collaboration for using elevators in hospital buildings. I received my PhD in Architecture from the Department of Architecture at Kyung Hee University, South Korea in August 2026, and was awarded the Graduate School’s Best Academic Award for the Department of Architecture. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology, Peshawar, Pakistan.

My research lies at the intersection of construction informatics, Building Information Modeling (BIM), extended reality, AI, digital twins, and construction automation. I develop semantic and interoperable workflows that connect BIM with game engines, immersive interfaces, robotic systems, and real-time decision-support tools for modular and industrialised construction.

My selected work includes BIM-to-Unity semantic data exchange, Ifc2Unity and IFCExplorer, ReUniXchange, BIM-linked mixed reality inspection and reporting, conversational LLM interfaces for BIM querying, 4D BIM simulation, and digital-twin workflows for immersive AEC applications.

Research Interests

  • Building Information Modeling (BIM) Semantic model data and open standards such as IFC
  • Construction Informatics Data-driven methods supporting decisions across the project lifecycle
  • Extended Reality (XR) Immersive VR and mixed reality for design review and site inspection
  • Construction Robotics Building models linked to robotic fabrication and on-site operations
  • Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DfMA) Modular, off-site production workflows for building delivery
  • AI for Construction Language and multimodal interfaces for querying building data
  • Digital Twin Schedule and sensor streams linked to live building models
  • BIM Data Interoperability Semantic mappings that preserve meaning between platforms
  • Robotic Navigation & Simulation BIM-driven indoor navigation and path planning tested in Unity and ROS simulations
